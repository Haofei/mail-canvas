Sense. Solve. Go.
How have we built our autonomous driving technology to navigate the world safely? With three easy steps: Sense, Solve, and Go. Using a combination of custom hardware and software, the Waymo Driver can visualize the world, calculate what others may do, and proceed smoothly and safely, day and night.

This summer we partnered with local artists to create artwork for our San Francisco fleet. Each artist depicted the magic of the Bay Area in a custom car wrap, and Waymo donated to their choice of a local nonprofit. Hear from artist Jessica HJ Lee on her process and the inspiration behind her work.

Going fully autonomous in Downtown Phoenix
We’re excited to announce that in addition to our fully autonomous Waymo One service in the East Valley, we’re now offering fully autonomous rides to Trusted Testers in Downtown Phoenix. We welcomed Phoenix Mayor Gallego on her first rider-only trip to celebrate this next step.
