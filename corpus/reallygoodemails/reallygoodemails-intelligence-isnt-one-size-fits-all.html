FEATURE HIGHLIGHT
 

How well do you filter out irrelevant information?

 

As you may know, Lost in Migration challenges Selective Attention, or your ability to suppress the impulse to pay attention to distractors and focus only on the target.

 

One way to measure this ability is to look at the difference in how quickly you respond when the flock of birds are all facing the same direction versus when they’re facing different directions. A smaller difference in response times might indicate that you’re doing a better job of ignoring the irrelevant birds and focusing just on the leader.
